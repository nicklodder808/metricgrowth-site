03How we do it

Four steps, from a list of names to a steady flow of referrals.

  1. Step 01

    Map

    We define your ideal client and map the professionals who serve them before and after you. You get a list of named firms, scored on how likely they are to refer.

  2. Step 02

    Recruit

    We approach each firm personally, explain what’s in it for them, and book introduction meetings in your diary. You show up.

  3. Step 03

    Activate

    Partners get a clear referral process. With your strongest ones we run joint plans: co-hosted seminars, joint guides and regular updates.

  4. Step 04

    Manage

    We look after your top partner relationships, track every referral and report honestly each month on where to invest next.
