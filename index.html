A partner recommends you.

03How it works

Six steps, from a list of companies to a steady flow of customers.

  1. Step 01

    Research

    We find the companies that already sell to your users, from software tools and CPAs to banks, associations and creators. Then we score each one.

  2. Step 02

    Offer

    We design the longer trial, discount or extra that makes their customers want to try your software.

  3. Step 03

    Deal

    We work out what each side earns, like a share of every subscription, so the partnership pays off for everyone.

  4. Step 04

    Partners

    We contact the best partners, pitch the partnership and negotiate the terms. You join the final meeting.

  5. Step 05

    Launch

    We build the partner landing pages, tracking links and onboarding emails that turn their customers into your subscribers.

  6. Step 06

    Growth

    We track every trial and subscription, improve the offer and add more partners. You get a clear report every month.
