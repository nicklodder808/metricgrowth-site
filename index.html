03How it works

Six steps, from a list of companies to a steady flow of customers.

  1. Step 01

    Research

    We find the companies that already sell to your customers, from banks and accountants to software tools, associations and creators. Then we score each one.

  2. Step 02

    Offer

    We design the discount, bonus or free extra that makes their customers want to try you.

  3. Step 03

    Deal

    We work out what each side earns, like a share of every sale, so the partnership pays off for everyone.

  4. Step 04

    Partners

    We contact the best partners, pitch the partnership and negotiate the terms. You join the final meeting.

  5. Step 05

    Launch

    We build the partner landing pages, tracking links and follow-up emails that turn their customers into yours.

  6. Step 06

    Growth

    We track every new customer, improve the offer and add more partners. You get a clear report every month.
